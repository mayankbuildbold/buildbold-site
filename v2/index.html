Pacework Crew

ClientPacework Crew
CityPune
Year2025
ScopeFull brand design

Three friends started a running club in Pune to pull people out of unhealthy habits. They needed a mark that looked like progress.

We drew a ribbon around the letter P, tilted it upward and turned its point into an arrow. Three lines run around it like lanes on a track. Then we put it on everything members wear and carry.

Trishakti Homestay

ClientAnubhuti & Anashuiya Bhattacharya
CityGuwahati
Year2025
ScopeLogo & stationery

A mother and her two daughters run this homestay. Trishakti means three strengths, so the mark is three figures under one roof.

Maroon and blush for the colours of an Assamese home, and a hand-drawn flower on every printed piece a guest takes away.

ClientArtifex Architecture Studio

An architecture studio working between modern and traditional design. Its mark joins the English a and the Hindi वे in one stroke.

Repeat the mark and it becomes a pattern, so the two scripts keep talking across every surface.

Pacework Crew

Full brand design, Pune, 2025

Three friends started a running club in Pune to pull people out of unhealthy habits. They needed a mark that looked like progress.

We drew a ribbon around the letter P, tilted it upward and turned its point into an arrow. Three lines run around it like lanes on a track. Then we put it on everything members wear and carry.

Trishakti Homestay

Logo and stationery, Guwahati, 2025

A mother and her two daughters run this homestay in Guwahati. Trishakti means three strengths, so the mark is three figures under one roof.

Maroon and blush for the colours of an Assamese home, and a hand-drawn flower on every printed piece a guest takes away.

Arch Veda

Logo and stationery, Bhubaneswar, 2024

An architecture studio working between modern and traditional design. Its mark joins the English a and the Hindi वे in one stroke.

  1. On grid paper

    The first a, drawn inside a square, with the studio's name lettered beneath.

  2. Two scripts

    Hindi notes in the margin: where the a ends, the वे can begin.

  3. One stroke

    The finished mark: a single line that reads both ways.

  4. With the name

    Set beside the name in lower case, wide and calm.

  5. Repeated

    Stacked and repeated, it becomes a pattern for every surface.

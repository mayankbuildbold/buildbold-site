Pacework Crew

Three friends started a running club in Pune to pull people out of unhealthy habits. We drew a ribbon around the letter P, tilted it upward into an arrow, and put it on everything members wear and carry.

Full brand design, Pune, 2025. Mark, colours, kit and merch.

Arch Veda, sketch to system

On grid paper. An architecture studio in Bhubaneswar working between modern and traditional design. The first a, drawn inside a square.

In one stroke. The English a and the Hindi वे joined in a single line that reads both ways.

Repeated. Stacked into a pattern, so the two scripts keep talking across every card and wall. Logo and stationery, 2024.

Trishakti Homestay

A mother and her two daughters run this homestay in Guwahati. Trishakti means three strengths, so the mark is three figures under one roof, in the maroon and blush of an Assamese home.

Logo and stationery, Guwahati, 2025.

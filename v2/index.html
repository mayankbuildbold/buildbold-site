Pacework Crew

Full brand design, Pune, 2025

Three friends started a running club in Pune to pull people out of unhealthy habits. They needed a mark that looked like progress.

We drew a ribbon around the letter P, tilted it upward and turned the point into an arrow. Three lines run around it like lanes on a track. Then we put it on everything members actually wear and carry.

Trishakti Homestay

Logo and stationery, Guwahati, 2025

A mother and her two daughters run this homestay in Guwahati. Trishakti means three strengths, so the mark is three figures under one roof.

Warm maroon and blush for the colours of an Assamese home, and a hand-drawn flower on every printed piece a guest takes away.

Arch Veda

Logo and stationery, Bhubaneswar, 2024

An architecture studio that works between modern and traditional design. Its mark joins the English letter a with the Hindi letter वे in one stroke.

Repeat the mark and it becomes a pattern, so the two languages keep talking across every surface.

How a BOLD project runs.

Clarity

Before we draw a line, we write a one-page brief with you: what we are making, for whom, and what it must do. You sign it. Every decision after is checked against it.

Structure

Every identity leaves with a guideline your printer can follow. Every building leaves with drawings your contractor can price. Nothing depends on us being in the room.

Intent

Each line in a BOLD logo and each wall in a BOLD plan has a reason we can explain to you in one sentence. If we can't, it goes.

Tell us what you're building.

Send the site location or plot size, the name of the business, and when you need it. We'll reply with questions and a first estimate.

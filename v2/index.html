01/ Brand identity — Pune — 2025

Pacework
Crew

Three friends started a running club in Pune to pull people out of unhealthy habits. They needed a mark that looked like progress.

We drew a ribbon around the letter P, tilted it upward and turned its point into an arrow. Three lines run around it like lanes on a track.

02/ Logo & stationery — Guwahati — 2025

Trishakti
Homestay

A mother and her two daughters run this homestay. Trishakti means three strengths, so the mark is three figures under one roof.

Maroon and blush for an Assamese home, and a hand-drawn flower on every piece a guest takes away.

03/ Logo & stationery — Bhubaneswar — 2024

Arch
Veda

An architecture studio between modern and traditional design. Its mark joins the English a and the Hindi वे in one stroke.

Repeat it and it becomes a pattern, so the two scripts keep talking across every surface.
